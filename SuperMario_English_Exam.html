Part 3: Reading Comprehension (25 marks)

The Mushroom Kingdom Tournament

Every year, the Mushroom Kingdom holds a exciting fighting tournament. Fighters from all over the world come to compete. This year, Mario, Luigi, and Princess Peach decided to enter the competition.


Mario has been training for three months. He wakes up at 6 o'clock every morning and runs five kilometers. "I want to win this year," he said in an interview yesterday. "I have practiced a lot since January."


Luigi is feeling nervous. He has never won a tournament before. "If I don't win this time, I will keep trying," he promised himself. His brother Mario is very supportive. "Don't worry," Mario told him. "You are a great fighter, and I believe in you."


Princess Peach is the defending champion. She won last year's tournament. "I have been preparing new techniques," she explained. "I will try my best to win again." The tournament will begin next Monday at the castle arena.

Questions 11-15: Choose the correct answer.

11. How long has Mario been training?

12. When did Mario start training?

13. What does Luigi promise himself?

14. Who won last year's tournament?

15. When will the tournament begin?

11. C (Three months)
12. C (January)
13. C (He will keep trying if he doesn't win)
14. C (Princess Peach)
15. B (Next Monday)

Reading 2: The Princess's Castle

Princess Peach has lived in her beautiful castle for many years. The castle has 100 rooms! Every room has something special inside. There are gardens with colorful flowers, libraries with thousands of books, and kitchens where chefs cook delicious food every day.


The princess loves hosting parties. Last month, she organized a grand celebration for the kingdom's heroes. Mario, Luigi, Toad, and Yoshi all attended the party. They ate pizza, pasta, and lots of cake. The party lasted from 2 o'clock in the afternoon until 10 o'clock at night!


"I have been planning this party for three weeks," said the princess. "I wanted to thank everyone for keeping our kingdom safe." She also announced that there will be another festival next season!

Questions 21-25: Choose the correct answer based on Reading 2.

22. C (Three weeks)
23. B (2pm to 10pm)
24. C (Bowser)
25. B (To thank everyone for keeping the kingdom safe)

Part 5: Listening Practice (15 marks)
Questions 18-20: Listen and choose the correct answer.

🎧 Practice Listening Script:

Mario: Hey Luigi, have you seen my red cap?

Luigi: Yes, I saw it in the kitchen this morning. You left it there after breakfast.

Mario: Thanks! Oh, by the way, the tournament is next week. Have you prepared your costume?

Luigi: Not yet. I've been very busy. I haven't had time to go shopping.

Mario: We should go together tomorrow. The shops will be less crowded in the morning.

Luigi: Good idea! Let's meet at 9 o'clock outside the castle.

18. Where did Mario leave his cap?

19. Why hasn't Luigi prepared his costume?

20. When and where will they meet?
